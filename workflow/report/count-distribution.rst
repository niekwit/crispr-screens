Read count distribution of sgRNAs per sample. A single peak indicates a healthy sample. A second peak at low counts (or a large fraction of sgRNAs below 10% of the mean count) indicates sgRNA dropout, typically caused by a bottleneck when too few cells were sorted or collected. In such samples MAGeCK gene-level LFCs can show discrete bands, because the median of the sgRNA LFCs depends mainly on how many sgRNAs per gene were lost by chance.
